# Discovery, metadata and citation sources for a JEPA landscape

Verified against provider documentation on **2026-11-07 UTC**. This is a source survey, not a coverage benchmark. No credentials were searched for or used. An OpenAlex key was mentioned but is not available in this folder; authenticated behavior was not tested. Discovery used limited Exa queries and primary documentation; no broad scrape was run.

## Recommended priority

1. **arXiv** for reproducible, current preprint discovery and version metadata; query explicit JEPA names and conceptual variants, not the acronym alone.
2. **OpenAlex** for a broad, reusable citation graph and cross-source identifiers. Supplement important seeds with **Semantic Scholar**, whose graph and matches differ. Neither graph is exhaustive or independent of the other.
3. **OpenReview** for venue-specific submissions, public decisions and discussions; **Crossref** for DOI metadata and deposited references. Check the actual proceedings for publication status.
4. **Hugging Face Papers** for community discovery and linked artifacts, and **alphaXiv** for supplementary ranked discovery. Their attention signals and rankings are not coverage guarantees.
5. **ACL Anthology, PMLR and CVF** for authoritative proceedings verification where relevant, rather than an assumed universal citation API: [ACL data repository](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-anthology), [PMLR proceedings](https://proceedings.mlr.press/), [CVF Open Access](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/). Venue records can lag submissions and are not a full preprint corpus.

## Provider details

| Source | Documented capability, IDs and coverage | Access, limits and reuse |
|---|---|---|
| **arXiv** | [Atom API](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/user-manual.html) searches metadata and accepts arXiv IDs; returned IDs distinguish versions, while the unversioned ID identifies the submission. Metadata includes publication/update dates, abstract, categories and sometimes DOI/journal reference. It is not a citation graph. | [API terms](https://info.arxiv.org/help/api/tou.html) ask for at most one request every 3 seconds and one connection at a time; cache and avoid excessive harvesting. API use does not grant rights to paper text: inspect each paper's license. |
| **OpenAlex** | [Works](https://help.openalex.org/api/) expose OpenAlex IDs, DOI, locations, `referenced_works`, citation counts and citing-work queries such as `filter=cites:W...`. Search and graph indexing are incomplete; arXiv locations and DOI links may or may not resolve to the desired work. | [Authentication](https://help.openalex.org/api/authentication/): keyless basic access, 10 requests/s; free verified key, 30 requests/s and cursor paging beyond first page. Free account budget $1/day, reset UTC; costs vary by request, inspect usage headers. Max 100/page and 100 OR values/filter. [Pricing](https://help.openalex.org/access/pricing/) says records are CC0, quarterly snapshot free, current sync filters paid. Full-text rights are separate. |
| **Semantic Scholar** | [Academic Graph API](https://api.semanticscholar.org/api-docs/graph) supports paper search, external IDs including DOI and arXiv, paper metadata, references and citing papers. The returned graph can include papers not in OpenAlex and vice versa; IDs and matches are provider assertions. | [API page](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api): most endpoints unauthenticated, shared 1,000 requests/s pool subject to further throttling; introductory key limit 1 request/s, supplied through `x-api-key`. Consult the [API license agreement](https://www.semanticscholar.org/product/api/license) and source-specific rights before reuse; do not assume all returned abstracts or text are CC0. |
| **Crossref** | [REST metadata](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) is keyed by DOI and comes primarily from member deposits. References may be absent, unstructured or lack resolved DOIs. Public citation counts do not establish a complete forward-citation graph; [getResolvedRefs](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/retrieving-identifiers-for-deposited-references/) is restricted to depositing members' own prefixes. | No key for public access; identify with `mailto` for polite pool. [Published limits](https://www.crossref.org/blog/announcing-changes-to-rest-api-rate-limits/): public single 5/s and list 1/s, concurrency 1; polite single 10/s and list 3/s, concurrency 3. Respect actual response headers and 429s. Crossref claims no ownership of individual bibliographic facts; abstracts may carry separate rights, as its [licensing guidance](https://www.crossref.org/documentation/retrieve-metadata/) explains. |
| **OpenReview** | [API v2](https://docs.openreview.net/reference/api-v2/openapi-definition) retrieves notes by ID, venue, invitation, forum and other filters. A submission note's ID and forum connect reviews and decisions; replies are discussion, not bibliographic citation edges. Current venues and most older ones use v2; [some older venues require v1](https://docs.openreview.net/how-to-guides/data-retrieval-and-modification/how-to-get-all-notes-for-submissions-reviews-rebuttals-etc). Include withdrawn/rejected status explicitly when relevant. | Public notes are readable without privileged access; note and field `readers` restrict visibility. Authentication is needed for restricted material. A numerical public rate limit and blanket reuse license were not verified; follow [terms](https://openreview.net/legal/terms), venue and content licenses, and avoid inferring that public reviews are unrestricted. |
| **Hugging Face Papers** | The provider's live [OpenAPI specification](https://huggingface.co/.well-known/openapi.json), inspected directly, lists `GET /api/daily_papers`, `/api/papers`, `/api/papers/search`, and `/api/papers/{paperId}`. These are documented in the spec, not merely guessed endpoints; check its current parameter schemas before use. [Paper Pages](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/en/paper-pages) are arXiv-oriented and link models, datasets and Spaces via repository-card references. They are not a complete scholarly citation graph. | Public reads versus authenticated operations depend on endpoint; see spec. [Hub rate limits](https://huggingface.co/docs/hub/rate-limits) vary by request bucket and account tier; honor headers and 429s. Paper, repository and community content rights vary; no blanket CC0 claim verified. |
| **alphaXiv** | Official [MCP documentation](https://www.alphaxiv.org/docs/mcp) documents `https://api.alphaxiv.org/mcp/v1`, `discover_papers` (ranked 5–15 candidates, arXiv IDs), hot/personal feeds, and paper-content tools. Search/analysis uses assistant quota; feed retrieval does not. `get_paper_content` defaults to an AI-generated intermediate report, not original text; `fullText=true` requests extracted text. No general citation-graph API or stable bulk REST contract was verified. | MCP advertises OAuth 2.1; headless clients can use an alphaXiv API key as bearer token. Numeric request and quota limits, data license and graph completeness were not verified. AI summaries and rankings require primary-source verification; paper rights remain separate. |

## Stable works, versions and provenance

Maintain an internal **work** identity with source records and explicit relationships, rather than making any provider ID globally canonical. Normalize DOI case and resolver prefixes; normalize arXiv IDs while retaining legacy IDs and version suffixes. Group `arXiv:xxxx.yyyyyvN` versions under the same unversioned arXiv submission, preserving each version's date and source URL. Link the preprint to a DOI, proceedings record and OpenReview submission only on strong evidence: explicit source links or consistent title, authors and date. A DOI can identify a distinct version or correction, so retain it as its own source/publication record even when associated with the same work. Similar titles alone are candidates for review, not automatic merges. Record source, retrieval date, matching evidence and conflicts; retain unresolved candidates rather than forcing a merge. Counts are source-specific and time-stamped, never summed across providers.

## Bounded citation expansion

For a reproducible initial pass, use a **chosen seed set** and expand only one hop in each direction, separately per seed. Suggested operational caps, not provider guarantees: up to 100 outgoing references and 100 incoming citing works per seed per graph source, maximum 500 newly discovered unique work candidates for the whole pass. Sort or sample explicitly, record filters, sort order, page limits, source counts and truncation. Prefer title/abstract relevance and a mix of recent and established work over citation-count ranking alone. Deduplicate candidates before review; do not recursively expand unreviewed candidates. A second hop, if needed, should use separately selected high-relevance works and a fresh explicit budget.

Caps create selection bias, particularly for high-citation or recent works; newest papers can have missing edges and counts. Missing edges are unknown, not negative evidence. Store observed direction and provider provenance for each edge and manually check influential claims against the original paper's references or the cited text. Cache responses, identify the client where requested, respect provider concurrency, and back off on 429/`Retry-After`.
